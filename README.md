# Asyntai AI Assistant for Omeka S

Adds the [Asyntai](https://asyntai.com) AI chatbot to the public pages of your
Omeka S sites. The chat answers your visitors from your own collections and
pages, day and night, in over 80 languages.

## Features

- The chat appears on every public page, in every theme. The admin pages stay clean.
- Set one chat for all sites, or a different chat for each site.
- Switch the chat off for one site.
- Show the chat to guests only.
- The chat loads after the page, so your visitors see your page first.

## Installation

1. Download the latest release ZIP and unpack it into the `modules` folder, so
   that the files are in `modules/Asyntai`.
2. Open **Modules** in the Omeka S admin and press **Install**.
3. Press **Configure** and paste your widget ID from your
   [Asyntai dashboard](https://asyntai.com/dashboard/). The chat appears at once.

For a different chat on one site, open the site, then **Settings**, then
**Asyntai AI Assistant**.

Full guide: https://asyntai.com/documentation/integrations/omeka/

## Requirements

- Omeka S 4.0 or later
- An Asyntai account
